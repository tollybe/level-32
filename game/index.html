STORY MODE · QUEST BRIEFING

What’s that? Your stomach is grumbling?

You’re on the hunt for food, and there is only one magic word you have been given that always leads to food… That’s right: SUCHT!

Okay, hero… Let’s help our furry friends and find Tolly!

Tolly has guarded the fridge with an unnecessarily powerful laser pointer, accidentally opening a tiny rift. Now a small army of squishy, rather stupid Bleeplings has invaded the gardens.

Mission: roam the sakura gardens, survive the Bleepling Menace and find three hidden clues to Tolly's whereabouts. Follow three mysterious pawprints, survive a sausage ambush and Boink & Bocht, then disarm three laser terminals during their green safe windows to uncover the story clues. Once you find Tolly, the Fridge Guardian boss battle begins. Dodge the Cone of Shame, rescue the chocolate, sausages and birthday treats, and recover the legendary laser pointer to unlock Mission Two: real-life laser tag!

Weapons: stuffed dragon toy, bouncing dog ball, sausage sword and Ellie’s frozen Kong dispenser. Nothing here is particularly sensible, but somehow it all makes sense.

For one hero: SUCH TOLLY! For two: SUCHT TOLLY! (German: “Search for Tolly!”)
